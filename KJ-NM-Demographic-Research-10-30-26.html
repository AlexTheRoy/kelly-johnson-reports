Demographic profile of all 356 populated ZIP codes in New Mexico, read through the lens of an Auto book: vehicles per household, how people get to work and how far they drive, household makeup, age and income. Everything comes from the U.S. Census Bureau's ACS 2020-2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), the most current ZIP-level data published. Section 06 zooms in on your top lead counties and ZIPs, and section 08 lines the market up against the 521 GOAL Auto leads on the account (leads dated June 22 to October 6, 2026). The companion spreadsheet carries every ZIP and every column; the Auto weather risk report covers hail, wind, wildfire and flood exposure for comprehensive coverage.

Why it matters for Auto
  • Miles drivenLong daily drives mean more miles on the vehicle and more reason to review roadside assistance, rental reimbursement and uninsured motorist coverage at quote time.

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020-2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), table-based summary file, tables B01001, B01002, B01003, B08201, B08301, B08303, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25003, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage356 populated ZIPs: every New Mexico ZIP (870 to 884) with resident population. PO-box-only ZIPs have no Census profile. Median income is published for 266 ZIPs and vehicles per household for 349. 4 of your Auto leads used a ZIP without a Census profile and are left out of the ZIP tables.
  • VehiclesVehicles available per household = (1 × one-vehicle households + 2 × two + 3 × three + 4 × four or more) ÷ households, from B08201. Counting “4 or more” as 4 makes it a slight floor. The Census counts vehicles kept at home for household use, including company vehicles.
  • GeographyEach ZIP is assigned to the county holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file), and counties are grouped into six regions: Albuquerque Metro (Bernalillo, Sandoval, Valencia, Torrance), Santa Fe & North Central, Northwest, Las Cruces & Southwest, Southeast and the Eastern Plains. Area names in section 06 are the city named most often on your lead records.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesKJ-NM-ZIP-Data-10-30-26.csv · every ZIP, every column, with the Auto weather tier from the companion report and your Auto lead and sold counts by ZIP.
